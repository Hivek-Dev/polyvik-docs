## The key fact

**The tool is Omni Flash 1.1.** It shows up only in a tooltip of the screen recording at second 52, not in the caption or hashtags.

The central claim: **3–4x cheaper than Seedance 2.5**, and overall "5x faster + 4x cheaper = way more ads".

## Flow shown on screen

1. **~45 s:** pastes a long prompt block next to a reference image.
2. **~52 s:** picks *Omni Flash 1.1* as the generation model.
3. **Result:** UGC avatars talking to camera. Three different ones are shown: a blonde woman, an older man and a brunette woman.
